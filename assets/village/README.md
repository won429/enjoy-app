# Drop Village assets

`grass-albedo.png` and `leaf-albedo.png` are original ImageGen textures generated for this project on 2026-11-02. Leaf alpha is used for curved, instanced foliage geometry and the shadow depth pass. The grass texture tiles on the subdivided terrain.

Architecture, player, clothing texture, bark texture, furniture and plant models are created in `village-scene.js`. No Nintendo game assets, character models or audio files are bundled.

Visual reference: Nintendo of America, *Animal Crossing: New Horizons Direct 2.20.2020*, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie4WZCLbtVs . The outdoor nature sequence around 2:30 and outdoor crafting sequence after 5:33 were viewed in the browser. Reference captures and observations are local QA material under `.local-preview/`.
